My BS thesis developed an age-estimation platform for skeletal remains based on Transition Analysis 3 (TA3), supervised by Assoc. Prof. Luís Cruz-Filipe at the University of Southern Denmark. The thesis received the top grade.

I continued as a Student Software Developer on the project through the NIJ-TA3 ADBOU / SDU collaboration, a joint effort between SDU and the University of Pennsylvania funded by the US National Institute of Justice, co-developing the cross-platform desktop application.
