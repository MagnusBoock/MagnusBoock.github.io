My MS thesis explored using Generative Flow Networks (GFlowNets) to generate realistic artificial spatial transcriptomics tissue data. Supervised by Prof. Richard Röttger and Prof. Melih Kandemir at the University of Southern Denmark. The thesis received the top grade (weighted average 10.67/12, GPA-converted 3.76/4).
